Ryota Yamazaki

Pianist

Biography

Ryota Yamazaki is a pianist from Tokyo, and he started playing piano at the age of seven.In 2023, he received Third Prize and the junior jury prize at the 64th Ferruccio Busoni International Piano Competition. In 2025, he participated in the 19th International Chopin Competition and the 17th Van Cliburn International Piano Competition.
